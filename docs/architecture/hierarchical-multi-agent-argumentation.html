R(xi, s, t) = f (C(xi, t), S(xi, t), Is(t), Qt).
f is a reasoning mechanism to be investigated, not a predefined scoring function
•
C(xi, t): incompatibility with valid reference knowledge
•
S(xi, t): observable potential to direct later interaction
•
Is(t): interests and context of stakeholders s
•
Qt: local reasoning states, collective interpretations, and unresolved disagreements
•
R(xi, s, t): traceable assessment, not an actual-harm prediction
•
Tt: retained traces of arguments, viewpoints, evidence, and remaining disagreement
